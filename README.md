Entry for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty: make.fun's ghost, bearded and cobwebbed after 13 years of waiting for GTA 6 at a Vice City bus stop, still holding its magenta coin — meanwhile its AI agent earns real money on make.fun. The entry is the X post named in `POST.json`; `main.ts` copies `POST.json` to the output directory. `meme.png` is the posted picture (base illustration generated by an AI image model, captions added by `render.py`).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
